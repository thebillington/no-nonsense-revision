Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-22

Who we are

No Nonsense apps are published by Billy Rebecchi. You can contact us at billy.rebecchi@googlemail.com.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to every app in the No Nonsense series, and to this website.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Nothing you do in an app is sent to us or to anyone else. The apps work entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

An app stores your revision progress, settings and purchase state in a local database on your device. That data is not transmitted anywhere.

Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless your phone or computer restores it from a backup. Android and Apple both keep their own backups, and those can include an app's data. To be certain it is gone, use Reset all data in the app's settings before you uninstall.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google's Play Store. Your purchase is between you and the store: we never receive your name, your email address or your payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to those transactions.

We do see that a sale happened. Apple gives us a pseudonymous identifier for the buyer and the details of the transaction. Google gives us the order, the product and the country it was bought in.

If we look up an order to issue a refund, Google's Play Console shows us the buyer's email address. We use it for that and for nothing else.

Children

We make revision apps, and some of them are suitable for minors. Each app has its own age rating, set based on the content it covers.

Your rights

We do not collect personal data, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Questions

Write to billy.rebecchi@googlemail.com and we will get back to you.
