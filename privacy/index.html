Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-19

Who we are

No Nonsense apps are published by Billy Rebecchi. Our postal address is available on request. You can contact us at billy.rebecchi@googlemail.com.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to every app in the No Nonsense series, and to this website.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Nothing you do in an app is sent to us or to anyone else. The apps work entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

An app stores your revision progress, settings and purchase state in a local database on your device. That data is not transmitted anywhere, and removing the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but we never receive your payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to those transactions.

Children

The apps are revision aids for qualifications and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will investigate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Questions

Write to billy.rebecchi@googlemail.com and we will get back to you.
